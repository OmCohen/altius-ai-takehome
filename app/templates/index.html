Ask about valuations, strategy, NAV, subscription credit facilities, or cross-quarter changes. The app will cite the source summaries it used.

How it works

The app loads the markdown summaries, ranks the most relevant sections with TF-IDF, and optionally calls an LLM to turn those passages into a short answer.

What you get

Each answer includes cited source files and reporting periods. If the corpus does not support the question, the app says so rather than inventing an answer.
