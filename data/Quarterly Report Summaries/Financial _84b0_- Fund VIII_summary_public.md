## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
The report covers an unaudited quarter for a large private equity vehicle investing in equity and equity-related securities alongside affiliated co-investment structures and alternative investment vehicles. During the quarter, net asset value declined modestly and the fund recorded a net operating deficit, driven by a large realized loss on one investment that outweighed positive unrealized revaluation gains elsewhere in the portfolio. Activity during the period included limited new investment outlay, several return-of-capital events, one add-on investment, and use of the subscription-backed credit facility.

### Strategy
The fund’s strategy is broad private equity investing across equity and equity-related securities. The structure allows investments to be made either through the main partnership, side investments, or alternative investment vehicles where legal, tax, regulatory, or operational considerations make that preferable, while preserving equivalent economics for investors.

Current activity suggests a relatively mature portfolio posture rather than aggressive deployment. Capital contributed net of qualified distributions stands at just over 95% of total commitments, with only modest purchase activity in the quarter and multiple return-of-capital events. The fund continues to support selected portfolio companies through follow-on capital where appropriate.

### Risks & Stress
The clearest stress signal in the quarter was a full cost write-off on one portfolio investment, which drove a substantial realized loss on disposal. Portfolio valuation movement was mixed, with some assets marked down materially quarter over quarter while others appreciated.

The fund also employed its revolving credit facility during the period, ending the quarter with a net drawn balance after having no balance at the prior year-end. The facility is secured in part by uncalled commitments and bank accounts, creating reliance on continued LP funding capacity and covenant compliance, though the borrowers remained compliant during the quarter.

Valuation risk remains inherent in the portfolio because a significant portion of investments are unquoted and valued using models with significant unobservable inputs, including earnings multiples, comparable company analysis, and adjustments for leverage, growth, tax, risk, and illiquidity.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The financial statements are prepared under US GAAP, with certain disclosure exceptions, and presented on a combined basis with affiliated alternative investment vehicles because they are under common management and treated economically as part of the same investment program.

The manager values investments at fair value at least semi-annually. Unquoted investments are valued using private equity valuation guidelines and methodologies such as EV/EBITDA, EV/EBIT, P/E, asset-based approaches, recent investment price, or industry benchmarks, with adjustments for company-specific factors and marketability restrictions where relevant. Publicly traded securities are valued using market prices.

The fund is regulated in its domicile and has an advisory board consent mechanism for term extensions. The credit facility includes financial covenants, and the borrowers were in compliance during the quarter and through issuance of the statements. A sustainability-linked pricing feature reduced facility margins after submission of a compliance certificate.

### Performance (Censored)
Portfolio performance in the quarter was mixed and below the prior period’s trajectory. The fund generated positive unrealized gains on revaluation across the portfolio overall, but these were more than offset by a significant realized loss, resulting in a net deficit for the quarter and a modest decline in net asset value. Despite the quarterly setback, the portfolio remains valued materially above aggregate cost overall.
