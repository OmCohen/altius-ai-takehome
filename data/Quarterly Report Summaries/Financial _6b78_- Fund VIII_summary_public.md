## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
The report covers the quarter ended 31 March 2024 for a large European private equity vehicle that co-invests alongside affiliated partnerships. Net asset value increased quarter over quarter, driven by net unrealised appreciation across the investment portfolio. The fund remained in an active ownership and deployment phase, with additional capital invested during the quarter and no realized investment disposals in the period. Cash balances rose materially, supported by a draw on the subscription-backed revolving credit facility.

### Strategy
The fund invests in a wide range of equity and equity-related securities and is structured to acquire positions with the intention to resell for capital gain, typically within 3–5 years or when a suitable exit can be arranged. The portfolio spans multiple sectors including healthcare, logistics, digital advertising, consulting, leisure, veterinary services, professional services, claims management, consumer brands, food, software, beverages, gaming, industrial distribution, infrastructure technology, sports rights, freight forwarding, and medical technology.

The quarter’s activity indicates a still-deploying but selective posture: the fund made a new investment, completed follow-on investments in existing portfolio companies, and continued to use side-car vehicles for portions of certain investments.

### Risks & Stress
The fund uses a large revolving credit facility to bridge capital calls, finance new and follow-on investments, and pay management fees and expenses. This introduces leverage, interest expense, facility fees, and covenant compliance requirements, including maintenance of a specified debt ratio. The facility is secured by capital call rights and bank accounts under defined default scenarios.

Valuation risk is meaningful because most investments are unquoted and marked using models with significant unobservable inputs. Fair values depend on judgement around earnings, comparable company multiples, leverage, tax, growth prospects, illiquidity, restrictions on disposal, and other company-specific adjustments. Changes in these assumptions could materially affect reported values.

The fund may also use derivatives to hedge currency and other risks on significant transactions, creating potential market risk beyond the carrying value recognized in the financial statements. Foreign exchange movements can affect both investment values and realized outcomes.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The vehicle is a Jersey limited partnership regulated in Jersey and prepares its accounts under US GAAP, following investment company accounting. Investments are held at fair value rather than consolidated. The manager estimates fair value at least semi-annually, using quoted prices where available and otherwise applying private market valuation methodologies aligned with industry valuation guidelines, including earnings-based and asset-based approaches.

Management fees accrue at 1.5% per annum of total commitments, subject to offsets from certain portfolio company fee income and management fee discounts. Organizational expenses are capped under the partnership agreement. The partnership term runs to 2031, with extension options subject to advisory board and investor approvals.

### Performance (Censored)
Portfolio performance was positive in the quarter. Net asset value increased, and the fund generated a net surplus from operations driven by broad unrealised gains that more than offset management fees, professional costs, interest expense, and other operating expenses. There were no realized investment gains in the quarter, so performance was primarily mark-to-market rather than exit-driven. Overall portfolio valuation appears above aggregate cost.
