## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
The quarter reflected a broadly positive valuation move across a large, diversified buyout portfolio, with portfolio fair value increasing quarter over quarter and overall fund value rising. The portfolio remained largely fully invested, with no realizations during the quarter and only limited investment activity, indicating a mature deployment phase. Operating performance across many portfolio companies was generally resilient, with several businesses delivering earnings growth, while a smaller set of assets continued to face softer trading, regulatory pressure, or leverage-related constraints.

### Market & Macroeconomic Environment
The report describes a mixed macro backdrop. In Europe, growth is expected to remain modest, supported by lower inflation and falling interest rates, but offset by weaker investment and softer external demand. A major theme is the effect of new US tariff regimes on European exports, with baseline tariffs on many goods increasing materially and sector-specific tariffs remaining elevated in some categories. This is expected to weigh on exports, investment, and business sentiment across several European economies.

The UK outlook is described as subdued but relatively resilient versus peers, with easing inflation and lower rates expected to support household spending, though higher energy costs, tax increases, and trade frictions remain headwinds. Across continental Europe, uncertainty around trade policy, weaker industrial activity, and soft external demand are recurring concerns. In Brazil, restrictive monetary policy is slowing activity, though fiscal support is cushioning the downturn.

Sector commentary in the portfolio points to soft freight markets, regulatory changes affecting gaming, ongoing inflationary pressure in selected consumer-facing businesses, and uneven demand conditions across industrial and consumer end markets. Currency also remained relevant, with euro-dollar moves disclosed in the report’s FX section.

### Strategy
The manager appears risk-aware but still constructive. The fund is in a late-stage deployment posture, with most commitments already drawn and remaining capital available for investing relatively limited. The strategy continues to emphasize businesses with strong market positions, credible business plans, and opportunities for cash flow and profit growth, including through acquisitions.

Across the portfolio, the manager continues to support operational improvement plans, tuck-in acquisitions, commercial initiatives, pricing actions, cost optimization, and selective M&A. The tone is disciplined rather than aggressively risk-on: there is continued conviction in value creation, but also active management of underperforming assets, margin protection, and debt reduction where relevant.

### Risks & Stress
The report highlights several stress factors across the portfolio and macro environment. These include:
- Soft demand and excess capacity in logistics and freight.
- Regulatory changes affecting certain consumer and gaming markets.
- Distribution losses and market-specific weakness in a global consumer staples business.
- Margin pressure from inflation, energy costs, and central cost growth in some consumer businesses.
- One-off cash outflows and leverage increases in selected assets.
- Multiple compression affecting at least one technology services investment.
- Trade-policy uncertainty and tariff escalation risk, particularly for export-oriented European businesses.
- High interest rates and weaker investment conditions in several markets.

One asset is explicitly classified under a corrective action rating, indicating jeopardized recovery of principal and interest. A few other investments remain materially below cost, reflecting ongoing valuation stress or operational underperformance.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The portfolio was revalued at quarter-end using a methodology aligned with ASC 820 and international private equity valuation guidelines. Valuations are based primarily on applying appropriate multiples to operating metrics such as EBITDA, EBITA, profit, or revenue, then deducting external debt and allocating equity value across instruments. The manager also notes monthly portfolio committee reviews of trading performance.

The fund uses a capital call facility as an intra-year working capital tool rather than long-term leverage, with the manager emphasizing that it is intended to reduce the frequency of capital calls and facilitate transaction execution. The report also notes continued reporting alignment with ILPA standards.

On responsible investment, the manager updated its group-wide responsible investment policy and maintains an exclusions and critical risks framework for sectors or activities deemed incompatible or requiring elevated review. The manager states the fund operated in compliance with governing documents during the quarter.

### Performance (Censored)
Performance was broadly positive in the quarter. Portfolio fair value increased meaningfully, and the portfolio overall remained above cost. Several investments saw strong earnings-led valuation gains, with some also benefiting from debt reduction. A smaller number of assets experienced valuation declines due to weaker trading, higher leverage, or lower market multiples. Overall, the portfolio appears to be tracking positively on aggregate, though with dispersion between strong performers and stressed or impaired positions.
