## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
This was an early operating period report for a large private equity vehicle that commenced operations in late May 2021. During the quarter, the fund made its initial investments across four companies in healthcare, logistics, digital advertising, and consulting/technology-enabled services. The investment portfolio was carried modestly above cost at quarter end, but the fund reported an overall net deficit driven by management fees, legal and professional expenses, and financing costs associated with a large subscription credit facility. The fund had not yet called partner capital net of qualified distributions by quarter end and was relying materially on the credit facility to finance investments and fund expenses.

### Market & Macroeconomic Environment
The report includes limited market commentary. The only macro-specific discussion relates to COVID-19, which the manager states has had a potentially adverse economic impact across countries in which the partnership invests. The manager notes that valuation impacts have varied by location, industry sector, and other factors, and that there remains a high degree of uncertainty in determining fair value.

### Strategy
The fund’s stated strategy is to invest in a wide range of equity and equity-related securities, with the intention to resell for capital gain typically within 3–5 years or when a suitable exit can be arranged. The quarter reflects an active deployment posture, with four initial investments completed shortly after operations commenced. The fund also co-invests alongside affiliated vehicles.

### Risks & Stress
COVID-19 is identified as a continuing source of economic and valuation uncertainty. The report also highlights valuation risk inherent in private investments, noting that fair value estimates rely on significant judgment and unobservable inputs, including earnings multiples, comparable company analysis, and adjustments for company-specific risks, leverage, tax, growth prospects, and illiquidity. The fund also carries material leverage through a revolving credit facility used to bridge capital calls, finance investments, and pay fees and expenses, creating financing and covenant compliance considerations, although no default occurred during the quarter.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The financial statements were prepared under US GAAP, with the vehicle treated as an investment company under the relevant accounting guidance and therefore not consolidating underlying portfolio companies. Investments are held at fair value, with valuations performed at least semi-annually and based on private equity valuation guidelines where quoted market prices are unavailable. Valuation methodologies may include earnings-based multiples, asset-based approaches, recent investment price, or industry benchmarks. Freely traded securities, if any, are valued using market prices.

Management fees accrue at 1.5% per annum of total commitments, subject to fee discounts tied to certain portfolio company fees. The fund is regulated in Jersey. The partnership term runs to 2031, with extension options subject to advisory board and investor approvals. The report also outlines security arrangements tied to the subscription facility, including security over capital call rights and bank accounts under specified default conditions.

### Performance (Censored)
The portfolio was marked slightly above cost at quarter end, indicating modest unrealized appreciation on newly acquired investments. However, overall fund-level performance for the period was negative due to the weight of management fees, organizational and professional expenses, and credit facility-related interest and fees during the ramp-up phase.
