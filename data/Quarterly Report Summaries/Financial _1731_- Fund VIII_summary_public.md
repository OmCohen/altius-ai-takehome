## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
For the quarter ended 30 June 2022, the fund reported a strong increase in net asset value and investment fair value, driven primarily by unrealised appreciation across the portfolio. The portfolio continued to scale through new investments and follow-on activity, while also receiving some proceeds and returns of capital from existing holdings. The fund remained in an active investment phase, with a relatively low percentage of total commitments drawn and substantial remaining unfunded capital. Use of the subscription credit facility increased materially during the period, supporting investment activity and fund expenses.

### Market & Macroeconomic Environment
The report notes that the effects of the pandemic continued to create economic uncertainty across the countries in which the fund invests. Valuations across investments experienced a range of impacts depending on location, industry sector, and other factors. The manager highlighted continued uncertainty in determining fair value because of the evolving economic backdrop.

### Strategy
The fund’s strategy is to invest in a wide range of equity and equity-related securities, with an intention to resell for capital gain typically within 3–5 years or when a suitable exit can be arranged. During the quarter, the fund remained actively deploying capital, including several new investments, while also making follow-on investments and receiving selected return-of-capital proceeds. Overall posture appears risk-on and deployment-oriented.

### Risks & Stress
The main risk discussion centers on valuation uncertainty arising from pandemic-related economic effects across different markets and sectors. The manager states that fair value determination remains subject to a high degree of inherent uncertainty. The fund also uses a large revolving credit facility, and the borrowing structure requires maintenance of a debt ratio, although no default occurred during the quarter. Valuation methodology for unquoted investments relies on significant unobservable inputs and manager judgment, which can materially affect reported values.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The fund is a Jersey limited partnership regulated in Jersey and prepares its financial statements under US GAAP, with investments carried at fair value under investment company accounting. The manager values securities at least semi-annually and applies private equity valuation guidelines for unquoted investments. Valuation approaches include earnings-based multiples, asset-based multiples, recent investment prices, and industry benchmarks, with adjustments for company-specific risks, growth, leverage, tax, and illiquidity where relevant. Freely traded securities are valued using market prices. The fund may also use derivatives to hedge currency and other risks on significant transactions. The partnership term runs to 2031, with potential extensions subject to governance approvals.

### Performance (Censored)
Portfolio performance was positive during the quarter, with the fund generating a substantial net surplus from operations driven by unrealised gains on investments. Overall portfolio value increased meaningfully relative to year-end, indicating performance above cost at the aggregate level. Investment gains more than offset management fees, professional expenses, financing costs, and foreign exchange losses on non-investment items.
