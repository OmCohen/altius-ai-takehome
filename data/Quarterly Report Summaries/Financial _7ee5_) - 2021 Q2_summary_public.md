# Public Summary

## General Summary (Public)
This is the fund’s first quarterly financial report covering the period from commencement of operations through quarter-end. The vehicle is structured as a co-investment partnership within a broader private equity program focused on equity and equity-related securities. During the period, activity was limited to fund setup, fee accruals, organizational and professional expenses, and initial use of a subscription-style credit facility. No capital had yet been called from investors by period-end, and the fund reported a net liability position driven entirely by accrued expenses and facility borrowings.

## Strategy
The fund’s stated mandate is to invest in a broad range of equity and equity-related securities. The structure co-invests alongside affiliated partnerships within the same broader program. The manager indicates an investment approach consistent with acquiring positions for capital appreciation and exiting typically within roughly three to five years, or when a suitable realization opportunity arises.

## Risks & Stress
The fund had drawn on a revolving credit facility to bridge capital calls for investments, management fees, and partnership expenses. The facility includes a required debt ratio covenant, though no default occurred during the reporting period. Security was granted over investor capital call rights and bank accounts, enforceable following an event of default and acceleration under specified conditions. The use of derivative instruments is permitted for hedging significant transactions, with disclosed exposure to currency and interest rate movements that may exceed the carrying value recognized in the financial statements. The financial statements also note reliance on estimates in reporting.

## Fund Operations & Governance
The fund is regulated in its domicile by the local financial services regulator. The financial statements were prepared under US GAAP, with the vehicle treated as an investment company under the relevant accounting guidance and therefore carrying investments at fair value rather than consolidating underlying holdings. Management fees accrue at 1.5% per annum on total commitments, subject to fee discounts and offsets from certain portfolio company fees received by affiliated parties. The partnership term runs to 2031, with potential extensions subject to advisory board and investor approvals. The report also outlines standard policies on foreign currency remeasurement, derivative accounting, and allocation of gains and losses among investors.

## Performance (Censored)
Reported performance for the initial period was negative, but this was entirely attributable to startup-period expenses, management fees, professional costs, and credit facility costs rather than investment performance. The fund had not yet called investor capital by period-end and had not reported any underlying investment assets in these financial statements.
