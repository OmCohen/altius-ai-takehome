## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
The quarter was defined by continued deployment, including two large acquisitions sourced from an older affiliated vehicle, plus follow-on funding for existing holdings. The portfolio fair value increased meaningfully over the quarter, driven primarily by new investments and selected earnings-led valuation gains, while overall fund NAV rose modestly. The portfolio remains broadly diversified across business services, software, consumer, healthcare, logistics, industrial, and sports/media-related assets.

Operating performance across the portfolio was mixed. Several companies reported solid revenue or EBITDA growth, successful cost actions, or continued execution on value creation plans, while others faced softer demand, delayed market recoveries, regulatory overhangs, or margin pressure. A small number of assets showed more acute stress, including one investment marked to zero and another remaining in corrective action.

### Market & Macroeconomic Environment
The report describes a muted but improving macro backdrop in Europe, with lower inflation and falling interest rates expected to support consumption, though growth remains subdued. Tight financing conditions are easing gradually, but investment and exports are still constrained by weak industrial demand and policy uncertainty.

Key themes include:
- Sluggish European growth, especially in industrial economies.
- Falling inflation and monetary easing supporting household demand.
- Continued pressure from high debt-service burdens in some markets.
- Weakness in manufacturing and export-oriented sectors.
- Ongoing softness in freight markets, with delayed capacity exits slowing pricing recovery in some transport segments.
- Recovery in parts of advertising and consumer demand, though lower-income consumer exposure remains softer.
- Inflation and input-cost pressure persisting in selected sectors, with some businesses attempting pass-through via pricing.
- Heightened uncertainty around potential future trade tariffs and broader geopolitical trade friction, particularly affecting export-heavy economies.

### Strategy
The manager remains risk-on and actively deploying capital. The fund added new investments during the quarter, including large-scale transactions and follow-on equity into existing holdings. The stated investment criteria continue to emphasize businesses with strong market positions, credible business plans, and opportunities for cash flow and profit growth, including through acquisitions.

Portfolio strategy also reflects:
- Continued use of add-on M&A at portfolio company level.
- Operational value creation plans, including cost reduction, pricing, margin improvement, and commercial expansion.
- Selective support for businesses through additional equity where needed.
- Willingness to execute related-party continuation-style transactions with governance approval.

Overall tone is constructive and active rather than defensive, though with select portfolio interventions where trading has weakened.

### Risks & Stress
The report highlights several stress indicators across the portfolio:
- One leisure-related asset experienced a sharp deterioration in summer demand and was marked down fully, with lender discussions underway regarding restructuring solutions.
- A building materials-related asset remains in corrective action and at a minimal valuation.
- Certain healthcare and consumer-facing businesses are facing weaker macro conditions, slower topline growth, or regulatory scrutiny.
- Freight markets remain soft in some channels, delaying pricing recovery.
- Some businesses are experiencing margin pressure from higher labor, energy, transportation, IT, or central costs.
- A few assets saw valuation declines due to lower earnings, lower multiples, negative FX, or higher debt.
- The fund continues to rely materially on a capital call facility for intra-year funding, with borrowings representing a large share of unfunded commitments at quarter-end.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The report states that the fund operated in compliance with its governing agreement during the quarter. Valuations are prepared under a fair value framework aligned with ASC 820 and international private equity valuation guidelines, primarily using earnings or revenue multiples less external debt, with portfolio performance reviewed monthly by the portfolio committee.

Governance and operational points include:
- Related-party acquisitions during the quarter were reviewed and approved by advisory boards of both the selling and buying vehicles.
- The manager updated its responsible investment policy to a group-wide framework with exclusions and critical-risk screening.
- The policy requires escalation for investments involving higher-risk sectors or activities.
- Reporting is prepared in line with industry quarterly reporting and subscription line guidance.
- The manager continues to use a capital call facility as a working-capital tool to reduce call frequency and accelerate transaction execution.

### Performance (Censored)
Portfolio value increased strongly during the quarter, largely due to new investments and selected earnings-driven uplifts. Overall portfolio value remains above cost, and the fund is generating positive unrealized appreciation on the aggregate portfolio. However, net fund-level return metrics softened versus the prior quarter, indicating that gains were partly offset by costs, financing effects, and weaker marks in certain holdings.

At the asset level, performance is mixed:
- Several investments are performing above cost with strong value creation and healthy earnings momentum.
- A number of holdings are only modestly above cost.
- A few investments are at or below cost, including one fully impaired position and another near-zero valuation.
- Realizations remain limited, so performance is still predominantly unrealized.
