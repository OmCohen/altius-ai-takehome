## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
The quarter was characterized by modest net asset value stability despite a slight decline in portfolio fair value, supported by a broad mix of operational outcomes across the portfolio. The fund remained largely invested, with about 95% of commitments drawn and limited remaining capital available for new investments. Activity during the period was concentrated on a small follow-on investment, several partial returns of capital from existing holdings, and the completion of one previously impaired loss realization through a lender transfer.

Portfolio performance was mixed. Several businesses delivered strong earnings growth and valuation uplifts, particularly in consumer, software, education, and gaming-related holdings, while a number of assets experienced earnings pressure, multiple compression, negative foreign exchange effects, or weaker end-market conditions. Overall fair value remained above residual cost, but quarterly gross and net IRRs declined versus prior quarters.

### Market & Macroeconomic Environment
The report describes a macro backdrop of weak but positive growth across much of Europe, with lower inflation and falling interest rates expected to support consumption, while investment remains constrained by uncertainty. A major theme is trade disruption and tariff risk tied to US policy, with concern that blanket and sector-specific tariffs could weigh on exports, business confidence, and capital spending across Europe.

The environment remains uneven by region:
- The euro area is expected to grow weakly, with softer investment offsetting some benefit from easing inflation and rates.
- The UK saw stronger early-year growth partly from export front-loading ahead of tariffs, but more recent data point to slowing activity.
- Several continental European economies face subdued growth due to trade uncertainty, weak external demand, high energy costs, and structural competitiveness issues.
- In selected non-European markets, high interest rates are expected to slow consumption and investment, though fiscal support and sector-specific strength may cushion the impact.

Foreign exchange was also a valuation factor during the quarter, with negative FX movements cited as a contributor to several markdowns.

### Strategy
The manager continues to emphasize investing in businesses with strong market positions, credible business plans, and opportunities for cash flow and profit growth, including through acquisitions. Current posture appears selective and disciplined rather than aggressively risk-on, reflecting limited remaining investable capital and a focus on portfolio management, operational improvement, tuck-in acquisitions, and selective follow-on funding.

Within the portfolio, the manager highlights continued execution of value creation plans, commercial initiatives, pricing actions, cost optimization, operational improvements, and M&A where appropriate. Several companies are pursuing add-ons or integration programs, while others are focused on turnaround or stabilization efforts.

### Risks & Stress
The report identifies multiple stress factors across the portfolio:
- Persistent market softness in logistics and industrial-linked activity.
- Regulatory pressure affecting certain consumer-facing and gaming-related businesses.
- Weaker macro conditions affecting topline growth and profitability in healthcare and discretionary consumer segments.
- Distribution losses, volume weakness, and market-specific challenges in consumer products.
- Earnings declines and debt increases at certain assets.
- Multiple compression in selected holdings, especially in discretionary consumer categories.
- FX headwinds affecting valuations.
- One investment was fully written off following transfer to lenders, after having previously been marked to zero.
- One current investment is explicitly classified under a corrective action rating, indicating recovery risk.

The report also notes that uncertainty around tariffs, trade policy, and external demand could further pressure investment activity and growth.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The report is unaudited and prepared in two volumes, with financial statements and capital accounts provided separately. Valuations are stated to follow ASC 820 and international private equity valuation guidelines, primarily using market-based multiples applied to relevant financial metrics, less external debt, with allocation across security classes as applicable. The portfolio is reviewed monthly by the portfolio committee for trading performance and valuation adjustments are reflected in quarter-end reporting.

The manager updated its responsible investment policy through a group-wide framework that includes exclusions and critical-risk sectors. Potential investments in excluded activities are screened out, while critical-risk areas require escalation and consultation before investment decisions proceed.

The fund reported full compliance with its partnership agreement for the quarter. Operationally, the manager also disclosed use of a capital call facility as an intra-year working capital tool rather than long-term leverage, with transparency around facility costs and annual drawdown practices.

### Performance (Censored)
Portfolio performance remained positive overall on a cumulative basis, with fair value still materially above cost, but quarter-over-quarter valuation movement was slightly negative. The portfolio appears broadly above cost and still value-accretive overall, though performance is mixed across assets.

A number of holdings are performing strongly and generating meaningful value creation, while others are below underwriting or under pressure from weaker earnings, debt increases, market softness, or lower valuation multiples. Net and gross return measures declined from prior quarters, suggesting some moderation in overall performance momentum.
