## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
The report is an unaudited quarterly financial statement package for a large private equity co-investment partnership for the quarter ended 30 June 2023. Net asset value increased versus year-end, driven primarily by additional investment activity and net unrealised appreciation. The portfolio remained heavily invested, with more than half of total commitments contributed net of qualified distributions. During the quarter, the fund continued deploying capital through new investments and add-on investments, while also using its subscription-backed credit facility materially more than at year-end.

### Strategy
The fund invests in a broad range of equity and equity-related securities and is structured to co-invest alongside affiliated vehicles. The stated holding approach is to acquire investments with the intention to resell for capital gain, typically within 3–5 years or when a suitable exit can be arranged. Activity during the quarter indicates an actively deploying, risk-on posture, including new platform investments and follow-on capital into existing positions.

### Risks & Stress
The fund uses a large revolving credit facility to bridge capital calls, finance new and follow-on investments, and pay management fees and partnership expenses. Borrowings increased sharply during the period, increasing leverage at the fund level. The facility is subject to a required debt ratio covenant, though no default occurred during the quarter. Valuation risk remains meaningful because fair value for unquoted investments is based on models using significant unobservable inputs, including earnings multiples, comparable company analysis, and adjustments for company-specific risks, leverage, tax, growth, and illiquidity. Derivative instruments may also be used for hedging and can involve market risk in excess of recorded carrying values.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The partnership is regulated in its domicile and prepares its accounts under US GAAP, with investment company accounting treatment. Investments are carried at fair value and are not consolidated. The general partner is required to estimate fair value at least semi-annually, using market prices where available and otherwise valuation approaches aligned with private equity valuation guidelines. Management fees accrue at 1.5% per annum of total commitments, subject to fee discounts and offsets from portfolio company fees received by affiliated parties. The partnership term runs to 2031, with potential extensions subject to advisory board and investor approvals.

### Performance (Censored)
Portfolio value increased during the quarter, and the fund generated a positive net surplus from operations despite a net investment loss from fees, interest, and other expenses. Performance was supported by broad unrealised appreciation across the investment portfolio. The portfolio overall remained marked above cost, indicating cumulative value creation relative to invested capital, although performance dispersion exists across individual holdings.
