## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
The report covers an unaudited quarter-end update for a large private equity co-investment vehicle focused on equity and equity-related investments. During the quarter, net asset value increased modestly, driven primarily by unrealized appreciation across the investment portfolio. The fund remained actively invested, making one new investment during the period, while no investment sale proceeds were recorded in the quarter. Capital returned to investors consisted of qualified distributions, realized gain distributions, and income distributions. The fund continued to use a revolving credit facility to bridge investment activity, fees, and expenses.

### Strategy
The fund’s strategy is to invest in a wide range of equity and equity-related securities with the intention to resell for capital gain, typically within 3–5 years or when a suitable exit can be arranged. The quarter’s activity indicates a still-deployed, active investment posture, with a new investment added and follow-through on an existing diversified buyout-style portfolio across multiple sectors. The manager appears operationally disciplined, using subscription-line financing as a bridge tool rather than as a sign of distress.

### Risks & Stress
The report highlights valuation risk inherent in private investments, particularly where fair value relies on significant unobservable inputs and judgment-based adjustments to comparable-company multiples. These inputs include earnings, leverage, tax assumptions, growth prospects, illiquidity, and company-specific risk factors, all of which can materially affect marks between periods. The fund also carries leverage through a revolving credit facility and is subject to debt ratio maintenance requirements, though no default occurred during the quarter. Derivative instruments may be used for hedging and can expose the fund to market risk beyond the carrying value recognized on the balance sheet.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The vehicle is structured as an investment company under US GAAP and therefore reports investments at fair value rather than consolidating underlying portfolio companies. Fair value is determined at least semi-annually by the manager in accordance with private equity valuation guidelines, using quoted prices where available and otherwise model-based approaches such as earnings multiples, asset-based methods, recent transaction pricing, or industry benchmarks. The financial statements are unaudited and prepared from the fund’s books and records for partner use. The fund has a defined term with extension options subject to advisory board and investor approvals. It is regulated in its domicile by the local financial services authority.

### Performance (Censored)
Portfolio performance was positive in the quarter. Net asset value increased, and results were driven mainly by unrealized gains on investments, partially offset by management fees, professional expenses, and financing costs. Overall portfolio marks moved upward quarter over quarter, suggesting the portfolio was broadly performing above prior carrying values during the period.
