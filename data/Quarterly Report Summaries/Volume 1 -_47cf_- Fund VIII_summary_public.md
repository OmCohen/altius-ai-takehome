## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
The quarter reflected continued rapid portfolio buildout, with several new investments completed and additional signed transactions expected to close shortly after quarter-end. The portfolio expanded to 17 investments, with invested capital reaching roughly one-fifth of commitments. Portfolio value increased meaningfully during the quarter, driven by new acquisitions and positive unrealized revaluation gains.

Operationally, the portfolio was generally described as performing well, with many businesses showing revenue and earnings growth, though performance was uneven across sectors. Consumer-facing and healthcare-related assets showed mixed trends, while several business services and technology-oriented companies reported strong growth. There were no realizations during the period, and no distributions were made.

### Market & Macroeconomic Environment
The report highlights a more difficult macro backdrop marked by slowing growth, elevated inflation, supply-chain disruption, high energy and commodity prices, and tightening monetary conditions. The war in Eastern Europe is described as a major external shock, particularly for Europe, with risks including weaker growth, stagflation, recession risk, and potential gas supply disruption.

Sector commentary points to:
- Ongoing supply/demand imbalances in freight markets, which had supported pricing and retention, though some normalization was expected later in the year.
- Continued digital advertising growth, but with emerging macro-related softness and some customer pullback late in the quarter.
- Inflation pressure across consumer and industrial supply chains, including energy, packaging, transportation, and raw materials.
- Recovery in travel, leisure, and elective healthcare activity as pandemic restrictions eased.
- Continued resilience in some long-term contracted media rights and recurring-revenue businesses.

FX also affected valuations, with both positive and negative currency impacts across the portfolio.

### Strategy
The manager remained clearly risk-on, continuing to deploy capital actively across a broad set of sectors including healthcare, software, business services, consumer, media rights, and industrial-related businesses. The strategy emphasized control and minority positions where appropriate, including co-control and preferred equity structures.

The manager also highlighted:
- Ongoing pursuit of add-ons and adjacent M&A.
- Geographic expansion and go-direct commercial rollouts in portfolio companies.
- Investment in digital capabilities, technology modernization, and salesforce buildout.
- Use of the capital call facility to fund most intra-year investment activity and reduce the frequency of LP drawdowns.

### Risks & Stress
The report identifies several portfolio and market risks:
- Inflationary pressure on input costs, wages, energy, packaging, and transportation.
- Recession risk and potential demand softening in cyclical end markets.
- Supply-chain disruption and labor shortages.
- Continued COVID-related workforce disruption in some service businesses.
- Margin pressure where companies are investing heavily in hiring, technology, or commercial expansion.
- Delays in customer implementation projects at one software business, temporarily affecting services revenue and EBITDA.
- Consumer demand softness and elevated cancellations in a leisure-related business, though bookings later recovered.
- Potential rate normalization in freight and weaker volumes if macro conditions deteriorate.

Despite these pressures, the portfolio was broadly still categorized as performing well.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The report states that the portfolio was revalued at quarter-end using a methodology compliant with ASC 820 and aligned with international private equity valuation guidelines. Valuations for unquoted companies are based primarily on applying appropriate multiples to earnings or revenue metrics, deducting external debt, and allocating value across the capital structure. Newly closed investments were generally held at cost.

The manager noted monthly portfolio committee review of trading performance and valuation implications. The fund was reported to be in compliance with governing documents for the quarter.

ESG content included portfolio-level data collection on emissions, renewable energy, board diversity, injuries, hiring, and employee engagement. The report also referenced responsible ownership initiatives at certain portfolio companies, including sustainability-related product design, employee policies, and formalization of ESG strategy.

### Performance (Censored)
Portfolio performance was positive overall. Fair value rose materially above cost, and the portfolio was marked at a premium to invested capital. Unrealized gains were the primary driver of NAV growth during the quarter, alongside new acquisitions.

Most investments were described as performing in line with or above plan, with several showing strong earnings growth and valuation uplift. A smaller number experienced softer trading, margin pressure, or temporary operational delays. Overall, the portfolio appears to be tracking ahead of cost and generating favorable early unrealized performance.
