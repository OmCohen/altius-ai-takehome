## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
This quarterly report covers an established private equity vehicle focused on equity and equity-related investments. The period was marked by a substantial increase in net asset value, significant capital deployment over the full year, and strong unrealised appreciation across the portfolio. During the quarter, the fund recorded positive net surplus driven primarily by valuation gains rather than realised exits or current income. The fund is now largely drawn, with contributed capital representing more than 95% of total commitments and only a modest amount of remaining unfunded commitments.

### Strategy
The fund’s strategy is to invest in a wide range of equity and equity-related securities with the intention to resell for capital gain, typically within 3–5 years or when a suitable exit can be arranged. The portfolio spans multiple sectors including healthcare, logistics, digital services, consulting, consumer, software, industrial distribution, education, gaming, and sports-related rights. The deployment posture appears advanced and largely invested rather than early-stage, with limited remaining unfunded capital and continued follow-on and new investment activity during 2024. Overall tone is constructive and risk-on in terms of portfolio buildout, though current-quarter performance was driven by marks rather than realizations.

### Risks & Stress
The report highlights valuation risk inherent in private investments, especially where fair value is based on significant unobservable inputs and manager judgment. Valuation outcomes are sensitive to earnings measures, comparable company multiples, leverage, tax assumptions, growth expectations, illiquidity, and company-specific risk adjustments. The fund also has exposure to foreign exchange movements and may use derivatives to hedge currency and other risks on significant transactions. The credit facility is secured by capital call rights and bank accounts, creating enforcement risk in a default scenario, although no covenant breach was reported. Some portfolio positions showed valuation declines versus prior year or cost, indicating asset-level dispersion within an otherwise appreciating portfolio.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The vehicle is a Jersey limited partnership regulated in Jersey and prepared its accounts under US GAAP, following investment company accounting. Investments are held at fair value and not consolidated. The manager estimates fair value at least semi-annually using market quotations where available and otherwise valuation techniques aligned with private equity valuation guidelines, including earnings-based and asset-based methodologies. Management fees stepped down in May 2024 from a commitment-based rate to a lower rate applied to acquisition cost of unrealised investments, subject to fee offsets from portfolio company fees. The fund has an advisory-board-based extension mechanism beyond its initial term and uses a revolving credit facility to bridge capital calls, fund investments, and pay fees and expenses. The borrowing base was reduced materially at year-end, and there were no outstanding borrowings at the reporting date.

### Performance (Censored)
Portfolio performance was positive for the quarter and year, with net asset value increasing materially year over year. Results were driven overwhelmingly by unrealised gains on investment revaluation, while realised gains remained minimal. The portfolio overall appears to be performing above cost, with aggregate fair value materially exceeding aggregate invested cost. Performance is positive but uneven across holdings, with some investments marked significantly above cost and others marked below cost or written down sharply.
