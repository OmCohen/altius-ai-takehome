## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
The report is an unaudited quarterly financial statement package for a large private equity partnership for the quarter ended 30 September 2023. During the period, net asset value increased versus year-end, driven primarily by unrealised appreciation across the investment portfolio. The fund remained actively invested, with additional capital deployed into existing and new portfolio positions, while also making a modest return of capital to investors. Borrowing under the subscription facility increased materially during the year, indicating continued use of bridge financing to support investments and fund expenses.

### Strategy
The fund’s stated strategy is to invest in a wide range of equity and equity-related securities, with an intention to resell for capital gain typically within 3–5 years or when a suitable exit can be arranged. Activity during the quarter indicates an actively deploying, risk-on posture, including follow-on investment in an existing portfolio company and a new investment late in the quarter. The fund also co-invests alongside affiliated vehicles and may use alternative investment vehicles for portions of certain investments.

### Risks & Stress
The report notes exposure to valuation uncertainty because fair values for unquoted investments rely on models with significant unobservable inputs, including earnings measures, comparable company multiples, leverage, tax assumptions, growth prospects, and illiquidity adjustments. Changes in these assumptions can materially affect reported values and operating results. The fund also uses a revolving credit facility and may employ derivatives to hedge currency and other risks on significant transactions, creating exposure to interest rate and market movements. The borrowing group is subject to a debt ratio covenant, though no default occurred during the quarter.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The partnership is structured as an investment company under US GAAP and therefore carries investments at fair value rather than consolidating underlying portfolio companies. Fair value is determined at least semi-annually by the manager in accordance with private equity valuation guidelines, using quoted market prices where available and model-based approaches for unquoted holdings. Management fees accrue at 1.5% per annum of total commitments, subject to fee offsets from certain portfolio company fees. The partnership is regulated in its domicile by the local financial services regulator. The term runs to 2031, with potential extensions subject to advisory board and investor consent mechanisms.

### Performance (Censored)
Portfolio performance was positive in the quarter and year to date, with gains driven overwhelmingly by unrealised appreciation rather than realised exits. Overall portfolio value increased meaningfully relative to cost and versus the prior year-end. The fund appears broadly to be performing above cost on aggregate, although performance is mixed across individual holdings, with some investments marked down relative to prior values or cost.
