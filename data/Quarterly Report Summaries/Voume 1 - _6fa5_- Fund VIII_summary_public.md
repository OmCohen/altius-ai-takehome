## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
This was the fund’s first reporting period following its effective date in late May 2021. The report is primarily focused on initial portfolio buildout, with multiple announced transactions across healthcare, logistics, digital advertising, consulting, insurance services, leisure, food, and consumer sectors. Most of these transactions were signed during the quarter or shortly thereafter, with several closing after period end. As of the reporting date, no capital had yet been drawn from investors for investments, and the fund had only utilized capital for management fees through a subscription facility.

### Strategy
The fund is pursuing a broad buyout strategy centered on acquiring controlling or significant minority stakes in businesses with strong market positions, credible business plans, and opportunities for cash flow and profit growth, including through acquisitions or strategic partnerships. The deployment posture appears risk-on, with a high volume of announced transactions shortly after the fund became effective and across multiple sectors and regions. The manager also demonstrated flexibility in structuring both majority and minority investments and in funding follow-on acquisition activity.

### Risks & Stress
The report notes ongoing uncertainty related to the pandemic and its potentially adverse economic effects across countries and sectors in which the fund invests. It highlights that valuation impacts may vary by location, industry, and other factors, and that there remains elevated uncertainty in determining fair value under current conditions.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The report states that the fund was operated in compliance with its partnership agreement during the quarter. Reporting is prepared in accordance with industry quarterly reporting standards and guidance on subscription credit lines. The manager emphasizes transparency around use of the capital call facility and describes it as a short-term working capital tool rather than structural leverage, typically intended to fund intra-year needs and be repaid before year-end. The facility was used during the period to fund management fees for a short duration. The report also includes organizational updates reflecting continued investment team hiring.
