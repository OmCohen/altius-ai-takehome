## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
The report is an unaudited quarterly financial statement package for a large private equity vehicle covering the quarter ended 30 September 2024. The fund continued active deployment during the period, with substantial new investment activity and follow-on capital invested across the portfolio. Net asset value increased meaningfully versus year-end, driven primarily by a large increase in the fair value of investments. The quarter’s operating result was positive overall, supported by unrealised valuation gains, though offset by management fees, professional expenses, and financing costs. The fund also made use of a large revolving credit facility to bridge capital calls and fund investments and expenses.

### Strategy
The fund invests in a broad range of equity and equity-related securities and is structured to co-invest alongside affiliated vehicles. The stated holding approach is to acquire investments with the intention to resell for capital gain, typically within 3–5 years or when a suitable exit can be arranged. Activity during the quarter indicates a risk-on deployment posture, with significant capital invested, including new platform investments and add-on investments. The portfolio spans multiple sectors including healthcare, logistics, digital services, consulting, consumer, software, sports/media rights, industrial distribution, education, and gaming.

### Risks & Stress
The fund uses a subscription-backed revolving credit facility to bridge capital calls, finance new and follow-on investments, and pay management fees and expenses, creating financing cost exposure and leverage-related operational risk. Interest expense and facility fees were material during the period. The facility includes a required debt ratio covenant, although no default occurred during the quarter. Valuation risk remains significant because many investments are unquoted and valued using models with significant unobservable inputs, including earnings multiples, comparable company analysis, and adjustments for leverage, tax, growth, risk, and illiquidity. The report notes that changes in these assumptions can materially increase or decrease fair values. Some portfolio positions showed valuation declines or low marks relative to cost, while others appreciated strongly, indicating dispersion across the portfolio.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The vehicle is a Jersey limited partnership regulated in Jersey and prepared its accounts under US GAAP, following investment company accounting. Investments are held at fair value and are not consolidated. Fair value is estimated at least semi-annually using market prices where available and otherwise with reference to private equity valuation guidelines and model-based approaches such as EV/EBITDA, EV/EBIT, P/E, and asset-based methods. The general partner may apply discounts for disposal restrictions where relevant. The fund term runs to 2031, with potential extensions subject to advisory board and investor approvals. The report also discloses advisory board approval for related-party transfers between affiliated funds during the quarter.

### Performance (Censored)
Portfolio value increased materially during the year to date and quarter-end net asset value was higher than at year-end. Overall performance for the quarter was positive, driven by unrealised appreciation across the investment portfolio. The portfolio as a whole appears to be marked above aggregate cost. Realised activity in the quarter was limited, with only a small realised gain recorded, so performance remains predominantly unrealised. Results were partially offset by a meaningful net investment loss from fees, professional costs, and borrowing costs.
