## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
The report is an unaudited quarterly financial statement package for a large buyout partnership for the quarter ended 30 September 2025. Net asset value increased modestly versus year-end 2024, supported by a strong quarter of unrealised appreciation that more than offset realised losses, management fees, financing costs, and other fund expenses. The portfolio remains substantially invested, with contributed capital near the high end of total commitments and a relatively limited amount of undrawn capital remaining. The fund also used a subscription-style credit facility during the period, with borrowings outstanding at quarter-end and a subsequent reduction in facility size announced effective year-end.

### Strategy
The fund invests in a broad range of equity and equity-related securities and co-invests alongside affiliated vehicles and alternative investment vehicles. The portfolio is diversified across multiple sectors including healthcare, logistics, digital services, consulting, consumer, software, industrial distribution, education, and leisure. The manager appears largely in portfolio management mode rather than early deployment mode, with the fund already substantially called and only a modest level of remaining commitments. The posture appears disciplined and selective, with activity focused on managing existing holdings, valuation progression, and financing flexibility.

### Risks & Stress
The quarter included a material realised loss on disposal of investments, indicating at least one exit below carrying value or cost basis. The fund also carries subscription facility borrowings, creating financing cost exposure and reliance on timely capital call support from investors if needed. The facility is secured in part by capital call rights and bank accounts, and enforceability provisions are tied to default and acceleration events. Portfolio valuation remains dependent on significant judgement and unobservable inputs for private assets, including earnings multiples, comparable company selection, leverage, tax assumptions, growth expectations, and illiquidity adjustments. Foreign exchange movements can also affect realised and unrealised outcomes.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The partnership is regulated in Jersey and prepares unaudited financial statements under US GAAP investment company accounting, with certain disclosure exceptions. The financial statements are presented on a combined basis with affiliated alternative investment vehicles where investments are economically treated as if held through the main partnership. The manager values investments at fair value at least semi-annually, using quoted prices where available and otherwise applying private equity valuation methodologies aligned with industry guidelines, including earnings-based and asset-based approaches. Management fees stepped down from a commitment-based rate to a lower rate applied to acquisition cost of unrealised investments beginning in 2024. The credit facility includes sustainability-linked margin adjustments following submission of a compliance certificate.

### Performance (Censored)
Portfolio performance was positive for the quarter overall, driven by broad unrealised gains across the investment portfolio. Despite this, the period also included a meaningful realised loss on an exited investment. Relative to year-end, overall value appears broadly stable to modestly improved, suggesting the portfolio is still carrying substantial embedded appreciation over cost, though with mixed asset-level outcomes.
