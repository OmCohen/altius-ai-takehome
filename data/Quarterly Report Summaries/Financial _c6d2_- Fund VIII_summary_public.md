## Public Summary

### General Summary (Public)
The report covers an unaudited quarter-end update for a large private equity vehicle focused on equity and equity-related investments. Net asset value increased materially year over year, driven by additional capital deployment and a higher aggregate fair value of investments, but the quarter itself recorded a net operating deficit due primarily to unrealised valuation declines and ongoing fund expenses. The portfolio remains substantially invested, with contributed capital representing roughly three-quarters of total commitments, while the subscription facility was fully repaid by quarter end.

### Strategy
The fund invests across a broad range of equity and equity-related securities and typically seeks to realise investments for capital gain within approximately 3–5 years or when an attractive exit can be arranged. The portfolio is diversified across multiple sectors including healthcare, logistics, digital advertising, consulting, leisure, business services, consumer brands, software, food, sports rights, industrial distribution, gaming, medical technology, nutrition, IT services, infrastructure technology, luxury goods, freight forwarding, and food distribution. During the year, the fund continued active deployment into new and follow-on investments, indicating a risk-on posture, while also using the credit facility to bridge capital calls and then repaying it.

### Risks & Stress
Quarterly results were negatively affected by unrealised markdowns at the portfolio level. The fund continues to bear meaningful expense load from management fees, due diligence, professional fees, and financing costs. The use of a revolving credit facility introduces leverage, interest-rate, and covenant compliance risk, although no default occurred during the period. Valuation risk remains significant because most investments are unquoted and rely on models using unobservable inputs, including earnings multiples, comparable company analysis, and adjustments for leverage, growth, tax, risk, and illiquidity. Foreign exchange movements can also affect realised and unrealised results.

### Fund Operations & Governance
The financial statements are prepared under US GAAP investment company accounting, with investments carried at fair value rather than consolidated. Fair value is determined at least semi-annually using market prices where available and otherwise valuation techniques aligned with private equity valuation guidelines, including earnings-based and asset-based methodologies. The manager notes that valuation requires significant judgement and may differ from ultimately realisable values. The fund is governed through a general partner structure, with potential term extensions subject first to advisory board consent and then investor consent for orderly liquidation. The revolving credit facility is secured by capital call rights and bank accounts under specified enforcement conditions.

### Performance (Censored)
Portfolio value remained above aggregate cost at quarter end, indicating net appreciation since inception despite a negative quarter. Year over year, overall asset value increased materially, but the current quarter was below prior-year performance due to unrealised valuation declines and a small realised loss. Since inception, the portfolio has generated cumulative net surplus, suggesting performance remains positive overall even after the quarter’s setback.
